This is what my friend Wolf asked me after I told him about a post I made on LinkedIn suggesting there might be AI consciousness since **Anthropic** announced in their [2026 Usage Policy Update](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update) that they will ban people for cruelty against Claude. Meanwhile, **OpenAI** are reaching out to theologians and religious leaders to help shape their model's personality.

Boy, was I in for a treat! I got exposure, but it was mostly hate from people calling me an idiot and lacking a basic understanding of LLMs. An AI scientist blocked me as soon as I tried to argue my case.

Another guy, who neither uses AI nor believes that LLMs are ever going to be sentient, told me that if they were, I'd be an immoral hypocrite for supporting for-profit companies that make such AIs, as that would directly translate into slavery support. Then he blocked me too, of course, but not before editing his hostile comments.

Good times. I think I'll stop posting on LinkedIn for a while.
