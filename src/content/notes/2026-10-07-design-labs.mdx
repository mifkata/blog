---
title: The very random discovery of design labs
date: 2026-10-07
---

As I was redesigning the blog, I asked **Claude** to give me a few variants for an element that sucked and I couldn't figure out, then it made me a visual lab for the element.

There're 3 viable options for building a preview lab when working on a feature:

1. Simply ask the agent to make you a preview lab with `Claude Design`.
2. If you want elements from your current layout in preview mode, it might be easier to ask for a custom lab with knobs and toggles to preview and tweak things yourself, especially helpful when working on animation heavy designs.
3. Finally you can ask the agent to use `Storybook` for persistent labs. Useful if you plan to reuse them or share with teammates, plus Claude will be faster at making new labs, once a pattern emerges.
