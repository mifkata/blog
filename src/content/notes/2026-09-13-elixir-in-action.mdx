A **quick recap** of how I got my **Elixir/Erlang/OTP** cherry popped and I'm never looking back.

I had a term project in uni and the prof wanted it in **.NET**, to which I vigorously objected. She said "What, if I made you write in Elixir? Would it be better?". Now, unlucky for her, she didn't know that I'd been wanting to get into **Elixir** for years now, so I did.

Wrapping my head around it in the [Exercism Elixir track](https://exercism.org/tracks/elixir) wasn't working for me, so I got this beautiful book. I'd like to express special thanks to Saša for writing it. It's been an absolute pleasure reading it.

If you're ready to get your hands dirty with what I think is the future of centralised, vertically scaling programming aka **The return to monoliths**, you should definitely look into **Elixir**. It has basically become my primary choice for my personal projects, as it's stable as a concrete wall and scales like magic with any resource you throw at it.
