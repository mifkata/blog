date: 2026-11-13

My favourite teacher at university used to say: "Before you use Artificial Intelligence, tame the Natural one". His words now resonate with me more than ever.

Creativity has been massively enabled by sheer supercompute, yet we get mostly shit. Hundreds and thousands of companies try to leverage the AI hype, yet they consistently do the same non-impressive, feeble compositions of what is known as AI slop.

It's puzzling, isn't it? AI enabled everybody to do more, better and faster, yet we just get more of the same.

Even when they figured out that it could reverse-engineer binary, they used it to replicate Photoshop and modify old video games.

It's the age of creativity. Don't be a copycat.
