title: Designing software factories is not an easy task
date: 2026-10-30
tags: []
---

I've been building a software factory for the last 40 days. Working 16 hours a day, 7 days a week. It's a churn like no other before. It's exhausting, it's painful.

I made various mistakes along the way and I'm starting to write an article about it today. Nobody should go through what I went through. Even though so many other people are doing it, it's hard to imagine many of them making as much progress as I have.

Not trying to brag, but unless you're a single, super-focused engineer, you're most probably screwed. Why? Because there's so much complexity to manage and there's no ultimate guide to solving it. So it requires constant refactoring. If this has to go through the standard work-loop: planning, engineering, reviews - all this in a 9-to-5 time frame... oh God, I don't even want to think about it.

As soon as you start working on a larger stack (a mistake that I made early on), you're already destined for slowness and long hours of debugging, reevaluation and reverification. Article coming soon.
