This week I rapidly exhausted my **Claude Code** allowance: 100% in 36 hours. This is the Pro x20 subscription. I've been wanting to try alternatives and of course I went for the 2 other big vendors - **OpenAI** and **Cursor**. Having used Cursor recently with Sol-5.6 in an environment where Claude seems to be malperforming, I was highly motivated. Oh boy, was I wrong.

I first got a **Codex Pro 100**. I gave it structured work that Claude followed immaculately. You'd think you'd see OpenAI steal some of the UX features of the Claude CLI. No, dude, its question tool doesn't work outside of `plan` mode, WTF?! Design - forget about it, the ugliest design lab I've seen in weeks. Tokens wasted in **6 hours**.

Time to test **Grok**. I recently read Elon Musk's latest biography (has 3 or 4) and gained massive respect for the man. I'm thinking "there's no way in hell this is bad". Truth be told, it seemed a fair bit better even than OpenAI's Astra. It did a bunch of dirty work for me, even bootstrapped a new project. I really can't complain. I even adopted **Composer 2.5** for small trivial tasks. In general not too bad, but in comparison to Sonnet (it's pointless to compare it with Opus), it feels ages behind. Following patterns is not its strong suit. Even when the prompt was literally "follow the pattern from X", it deviated massively from the target expectations.

The conclusion - these models are still far behind what Anthropic is bringing to the table. If you're choosing between vendors, don't waste money on either.

**Next**: OpenCode Go subscription for **Kimi-K3.1-Pro**, **GLM-5.3-Flash** and **DeepSeek-4.1-Flash**.
